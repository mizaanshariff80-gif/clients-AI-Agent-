## Outreach email format (Nexora AI cold-email template)

**Subject:** `Noticed something about your <industry> work`
(e.g. "Noticed something about your remodeling work", "Noticed something about your HVAC work")

**Body structure (plain text, 4 short paragraphs):**

1. **Greeting** — `Hi <FirstName>,` for a named contact, or `Hi <Company> team,` for a shared inbox.
2. **Personalized hook** — one specific, researched fact about the company (years in business, review score, review themes, brand promise) followed by the gap or risk it exposes: slow response to inquiries, missed calls, untapped existing-customer base.
3. **Pitch** — `I set up automated communication systems for <industry> — instant response to new inquiries, automatic status updates to active customers — so <tie back to their specific gap>.`
4. **CTA** — `Worth a 60-second example? Just reply "yes."`
